## Full description
Find your rhythm.

Lumyrinth is a private, offline-first breathing companion designed for quiet moments of calm, focus, reset, and rest. Choose a guided rhythm, follow the luminous breathing visual, and build a simple practice at your own pace.

FEATURES
- Guided breathing sessions for relaxation, focus, sleep, and quick resets
- Six ready-to-use rhythms, including Slow Down, Equal Rhythm, Square, Steady, Nightfall, and Quick Reset
- Create and save comfortable custom rhythms
- Original offline ambience: Rain, Night, Ocean, Forest, Fireplace, Stream, and Deep Space
- Gentle sound and haptic guidance, with controls during every session
- Private session history and a quiet progress view
- No account, advertising, cloud sync, or subscription

Lumyrinth is made for general relaxation and breathing practice. Breathe comfortably and stop if you feel uncomfortable or dizzy. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment.
